## About this portfolio

This site is my e-Portfolio for the MSc Artificial Intelligence programme at the
University of Essex Online. It is built with Jekyll and published with GitHub
Pages from the `master` branch of
[aabouraya/msc-ai-portfolio](https://github.com/aabouraya/msc-ai-portfolio).

Its purpose is threefold:

1. **Evidence** — to collect the artefacts each module requires for assessment,
   explicitly mapped to the module learning outcomes.
2. **Reflection** — to record what I actually learned, including what did not go
   to plan, rather than only the finished outputs.
3. **Professional development** — to track skills against a matrix and an action
   plan that carries forward across modules.

## Academic integrity

All work published here is my own unless attributed. Where an artefact was
produced by a team, my individual contribution is stated. Collaborative
discussion posts are reproduced as submitted, with peers referred to by initials
or first name only. Sources are referenced in UoE Harvard style.
