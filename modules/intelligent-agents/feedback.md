Feedback is recorded with the response to it. A log of comments received is not
evidence on its own; the evidence is the change that followed.

## Feedback log

<div class="wide" markdown="1">

| Date | From | On | Feedback | Action taken |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| *date* | *Tutor / peer* | *artefact* | *summary of the comment* | *what I changed, or why I disagreed* |

</div>

<div class="todo" markdown="1">
Include feedback you did not act on, with your reasoning. Disagreeing with a
comment and justifying it is a stronger demonstration of critical thinking than
accepting everything.
</div>
